Featured LX-1 thrust chamber

ethanol coolant combustion gas schematic, not to scale

LX-1 is our ethanol/LOX liquid engine and the core of the SKYLX propulsion program. The fuel does two jobs: before it's injected, ethanol runs through channels in the chamber wall, pulling heat out of the throat on its way to the injector.

Metal-printed chambers are expensive to get wrong, so before committing to an IN718 print I printed the chamber in PLA-CF and pushed water through it to validate the cooling channel geometry. Cheap test first, expensive hardware once.
