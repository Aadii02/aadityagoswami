About

I founded SkyLakes Aerospace and study mechanical engineering at IPEC in Delhi. We're building SKYLX, a family of reusable small-lift launch vehicles for the sub-₹40 Cr LEO market. The company is incorporated and registered with IN-SPACe.

I've always built things. As a kid I took apart toy cars and carried a briefcase of tools everywhere, which earned me the family nickname kabadi wala. My practical knowledge still runs ahead of my theory, and I'm fine with that. I'd rather build the test stand than read another paper about it.

SkyLakes started as a student project to put India's first student-built rocket past the Kármán line. It became a company once the goal outgrew what a student project could carry. The aim now is to make it Asia's biggest aerospace company.
