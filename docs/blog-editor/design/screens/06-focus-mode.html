Leonardo filled thousands of notebook pages with machines he never built. What survived wasn’t the finished work — it was the habit of looking closely and writing it down.

The workshop, not the to-do list

A to-do list tells you what’s left. A workshop shows you what you’ve made — the half-finished parts, the sketches, the things you tried and put down. One makes me anxious; the other makes me want to pick up a tool

So this blog is a workbench. Food, code and half-formed thoughts, left out where I can see them.
